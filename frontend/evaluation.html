EMPIRICAL ABLATION STUDY

Chunking Strategy & Re-Ranking: The Path from 78.5% to 95.7%

Retrieval quality in spoken technical lectures differs fundamentally from text articles. Spoken sentences drift, explanations take multiple minutes, and speech boundaries rarely align with semantic boundaries.

BASELINE ARCHITECTURE 78.5% Hit Rate@5
✕ Fixed 75-second Windows: Cut mathematical recurrences and base-case proofs mid-sentence at fixed time intervals.
✕ Zero Overlap: Inquiries targeting the transition between intuition and code failed when the context was split across consecutive chunks.
✕ Bi-Encoder Alone: Fast similarity missed subtle nuances (e.g. distinguishing 1e9 sentinel from INT_MAX).
Result: 21.5% retrieval failure on technical questions.
VIDQUERY OPTIMIZED 95.7% Hit Rate@5 (+17.2%)
✓ 55s Window + 15s Sliding Overlap: Guarantees that complete sentence units and logical explanations remain unbroken in at least one chunk.
✓ Cross-Encoder Re-Ranking: Performs full cross-attention over top-15 retrieved chunks, surfacing high-precision answers to rank #1.
✓ Cross-Lingual Hindi Translation: Bridges Hinglish spoken lectures with formal English technical queries.
Result: Sub-600ms latency with 95.7% top-5 retrieval accuracy.
